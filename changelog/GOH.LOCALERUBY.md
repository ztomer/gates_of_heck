* GOH.LOCALERUBY: `goh locale-prefix` reads the shell a Ruby source hands to a shell (`crates/goh/src/rubyshell.rs`): a heredoc passed after `"-c"` or to `system`, or assigned to a variable passed after `"-c"` (a cask's `uninstall` script), and the one string of a `system "..."` call. homebrew-tap's `Casks/divoom-control.rb` runs three `LC_ALL=C` prefixes in its `uninstall` heredoc; they are now read and judged. A caveat or any other heredoc no shell runs stays prose. The shape table's `.rb` rows name this item; `.rb` left the "language the scan does not read" row.
