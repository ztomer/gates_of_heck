* **A land runs its pre-land tests and its gate at once; the land costs the longer, not the sum (GOH.GATEPARALLEL).** Both judge the one rebased tree main fast-forwards to and neither reads the other, yet `tools/land.sh` ran them in turn: pre-land 255 s then gate 393 s, medians over 94 plain lands. Now the pre-step runs under the land lock beside the gate (`lib/land_par.sh`), each in a branch of its own with its own admission and deadline; the first red stops the other branch whole (`goh_pid_tree_kill`, `lib/pid_owner.sh`) and is the one named (`the pre-land went red first -- the gate was stopped`). Nothing lands unless both are green (a pre-land past its deadline stays no verdict); the land record keeps the pre-land's seconds. The gate's suite reads the enqueue's kept verdict, never the record the pre-land beside it is writing. `GOH_LAND_GATE_MAX` (default `90m`) names the gate's deadline. `goh land --enqueue` still runs the pre-step before queueing.
