* GOH.ADMISSION: a heavy job from any session comes in by the one admission. The Bash guard (`hooks/claude/discard_guard.py`) rewrites a cargo, swift, xcodebuild, gradle or go build, or a shellcheck over 4+ files, a glob or xargs, to take a gate slot first (`lib/heavy_admit.py take`): it waits behind the gates and other sessions' heavy jobs, says who holds every slot while it waits (each admitted job's holder record names its session and command), exports `GOH_GATE_SLOT` so a gate it runs nests, and frees the slot when the tool's shell exits. Past `GOH_HEAVY_ADMIT_WAIT` (300 s) it runs anyway and says beside whom; `# admit-ok: <reason>` is the escape.
