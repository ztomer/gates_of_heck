* **A land's uncovered changed lines are carried to the next ship: the land files their fix item, and `goh ship` refuses before its suite (GOH.DIFFCOVCARRY).** v0.140.3, v0.142.0 and v0.143.0 each went red at diff coverage after a 15-16 min suite, on lines a land had only warned about. The land's coverage warning (`lib/cov_risk.py`) now keeps `carry` on the land record: each line the ship's diff coverage would refuse, as file, line and text, with `own` marking the land's own commits' lines. The kept ship profile also records lines covered by the external suite, so those are no longer carried. A landing with its own carried lines files `GOH.DIFFCOV<sha>` through `loopctl file` (phase SPD, a regression, class GOH.DIFFCOVCARRY), so a fix lane can start minutes after the land. `goh ship` refuses in preflight, in seconds, while any land since the base tag carried a line that is still uncovered, naming each `file:line  text`. A line stops counting once it is deleted or edited at the ship's tip, once the item filed for it has landed, or once a later land judged on a tip holding it no longer carries it. `--allow-diffcov REASON` ships anyway and keeps the reason on the result (`lib/diffcov_carry.py`).
