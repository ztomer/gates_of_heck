* EST.TMPLEAK: every `local_ci.sh` step runs with a TMPDIR of its own, a reused `goh-step.slotNN` under `$TMPDIR/goh.noindex` (`lib/step_tmp.sh`). What a green step leaves there is named and removed, so a suite's leftovers no longer pile up in the user's TMPDIR (34,022 entries on 2026-11-14); a red step's temp dir is kept with its log. `GOH_TMP_LEAKS=red` in a repo's `.gatesrc` fails a leaking step, `GOH_TMP_LEAK_ALLOW` is its ratchet of known leaks (an entry that matches nothing fails), `off` restores the old TMPDIR. A step's PATH leads with an `sccache` shim that runs the server under the user's TMPDIR.
