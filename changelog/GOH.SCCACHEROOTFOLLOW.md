* GOH.SCCACHEROOTFOLLOW: the sccache server stays on the resolver's root. The cache sweep applies any standing difference once the server is idle, and after an hour with no idle window without one (`goh cache-sweep hold HOURS WHY` is the explicit, expiring opt-out, at most 72 h). A missing server is started on the resolver's root by the next compile (`lib/goh-rustc`) or gate (`lib/sccache_dir.sh`) instead of on the client's internal default; a drive that cannot hold it falls back to internal with the reason in the start log. The wrapper exports `SCCACHE_IGNORE_SERVER_IO_ERROR=1`, so a restart mid-build costs a local compile. The internal default dir no server serves is swept oldest-first to the cap, and below it while the internal tier is over its budget.
