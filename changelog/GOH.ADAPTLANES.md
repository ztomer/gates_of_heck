- `goh capacity [--json]` (`lib/capacity.py`) is the one reading of the machine's room for another write lane: free gate slots and live waiting tickets (the slots' own state), load per core, memory, free disk on the build volume, and the CPU weight of the gates in flight from the one timing record (`gate_weight`, for GOH.CPUADMIT). agent_guard applies its rule to every session's write spawns (`hooks/claude/lane_room.py`, `GOH_LANE_ADMIT`: `warn` by default, `refuse` once calibrated, a failed reading admits) and records each in `~/.local/state/goh/lane-starts.jsonl` (GOH.ADAPTLANES).
