* **A saved pid is signalled only while it still names the process it was saved for** (GOH.STRAYSIGNAL). A loaded full-suite land saw a fake build's `sleep 60` end after a second: the suite allocates hundreds of pids a second, so a pid kept in a variable or read from a pidfile after its process ended names a stranger within minutes, and 46 sites signalled one -- tests whose `finally` SIGKILLed a step they had just asserted dead (`test_bounded_run_interrupt.py`, `test_container_runtime.py`, `test_desktop_lock.sh` twice in a row), and the load reaper's TERM-then-KILL. `lib/pid_owner.py` (and `lib/pid_owner.sh`) stamps a process's birth (macOS `proc_pidinfo`, Linux `/proc/PID/stat`; `ps -o lstart=` for the shell and for a pidfile that carries it) and refuses a pid whose birth changed; `signal_child_group` signals a child's group through its handle, refusing once the leader is reaped and its number is a stranger's. 38 sites moved onto stamps or handles; `checks/check_saved_pid_kill.py` (a `GOH_CI_STEPS` step, `tests/test_check_saved_pid_kill.py`) refuses a new signal to a saved pid, with a ratchet in `checks/saved_pid_kill_allow.json` (6 legitimate, 3 bare left: `gates/local_ci.sh`'s two step TERMs and `tests/test_orphan_canary.py`'s snapshot kill).
