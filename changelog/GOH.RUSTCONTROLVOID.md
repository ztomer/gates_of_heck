* **The rust timing control is sampled during the gate, so a land's rust ratios are figures again (`GOH.RUSTCONTROLVOID`).** One control before a 7-25 min land gate and one after could not stand for the load under a step in the middle: across 115 lands (2026-11-17..19, `~/.local/state/goh/timing/ledger.jsonl`) the end-to-end drift followed ln(load_end / load_start) (r 0.57, about +10% per e-fold of load) and voided 31 (27%), 20 of them among the 57 where the load moved more than 2x. `tools/gate_profile.sh` now also runs `lib/step_control.py watch`, one control every 30 s while the gate runs (measured: 30 s apart, six moves at load 20 -> 134 all within 15%, worst +14.1%), reaped with the gate; every timings row carries its end time `t`, and each rust step is divided by the median of the controls around it, void only when fewer than five of them spread more than 15%, and then the line names the spread and the load at each end. Controls take a box-wide lock (every land builds the same four cached copies), record their 1-min load, and a sampler stopped mid-control kills its clippies. The end-to-end drift is still printed and still the ledger's `control_drift`, so `goh budget`'s raw land cpu-s keeps its void. Rows with no `t` (older timings) keep the two-ends rule.
