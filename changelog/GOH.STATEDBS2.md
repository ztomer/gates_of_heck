* GOH.STATEDBS2: every step's rewrite of a ship record writes its goh.db row first (`lib/ship_status.py` save; the install's pins likewise), and every goh reader of ship records reads the rows: `goh ship --status` and its worker, `goh ship verdict`, the land queue's ships in flight, `goh budget` (backfill and the unmeasured repos), the ship lock's holder line, the push sweep's starter, the pin carry. A result with no row (written before the switch, or by an old goh) is read from its file, and `goh ship` imports such files at its start.
