* Landing queue: a pending land that conflicts with a land that just moved main is red at once, not when its turn comes (`lib/landq_early.py`, GOH.EARLYCONFLICT). After every lander pass each pending land is replayed onto main in objects only (`git merge-tree --write-tree` per own commit, with land.sh's row merge driver; no worktree checked out, no gate run) and a conflicting one gets the batch's record (`conflict: did not stack on <main>`) and a log naming the files and the commits on main since its fork that touch them. `goh land --enqueue --land` judges its tip the same way before filing it: a conflicting one is recorded red, never queued, and the enqueue exits 1. Judged against main only, since a conflict with a request still pending is no verdict (it may go red); the batch still reds one that does not stack on the item below it. Cost: about 240 ms per pending item on gates_of_heck (2-commit lanes, replaying GOH.CRATEFLOOR's and GOH.PRELANDTWICE's 2026-11-18 tips, which reproduce their conflicts in gates/lcov_merge.py and tools/landq.py + lib/land_prestep.py); 2026-11-18 those two waited ~40 min to learn it.
