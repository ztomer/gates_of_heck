- fix(land): a re-enqueue of an item supersedes the earlier enqueue's pre-step, and `goh land --cancel ITEM` does the same on demand (GOH.REENQUEUECANCEL). The in-flight marker now records the pre-step's process group as stamped at its spawn and the checkout slot it claimed; `lib/landq_supersede.py` puts a tombstone beside the marker, stops the enqueuer and the pre-step's group by their recorded pid and birth (TERM, then KILL; never by name), then frees the slot. An enqueuer that finds its tombstone files nothing, so no request for the replaced sha is ever queued. Before this, an orphaned pre-step held one of the two gate slots for up to 25 min and could later file a request for a sha its lane had already replaced.
