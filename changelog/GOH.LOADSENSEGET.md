* GOH.LOADSENSEGET: `tests/load_sensitive.py` no longer reads a dict lookup with a default (`seed.get(name, 0)`) as a bounded wait. A `get` or `acquire` waits by its `timeout=` keyword, or by a number after a literal block flag (`queue.get(True, 1)`, `lock.acquire(True, 1)`); `wait`, `join` and `communicate` keep any literal argument as their bound. The false positive had forced 12 bare `load_sensitive` marks onto tests that never wait (seed ratchets, scans); ten are dropped and the two with a stated cause (`tests/test_goh_rustc.py`, `tests/test_goh_rustc_paths.py`: a private sccache server and real compiles) now carry it as `reason=`.
