* **A ruff newer than goh's pin starts its own bump-only land** (GOH.RUFFBUMPLAND). `lib/ruff_bump.py`: when the box's newest ruff is past the pin on main's tip, no land is pending, running or in its pre-step, and no goh ship is in flight, it commits the bump alone (`gates/ruff_currency.py`'s bump) on a fresh worktree of main and enqueues it through the normal queue as `GOH.RUFF<digits>`, once per release (`<landq>/ruff-bump/<ver>.json`). The lander's idle pass runs it, and so does `goh land --ruff-bump --detach`, which a consumer gate's `goh_py_ruff_pinned` starts the moment it meets the newer ruff: the estate is red for one land, not until somebody's next goh land. `tests/test_ruff_bump.py`.
