* GOH.SUITEDRIFTBUILD: the suite's per-test ceiling (tests/_drift_guard.py) no longer reds a CPU-bound test that a lane or work gate's QoS clamp (`taskpolicy -c utility`) slowed. The run queue cannot see the clamp: a clamped thread ran a fixed loop x25-35 slower while the load average said x3.2. A phase is judged by the smaller of its wall over the run queue's stretch and its CPU at the stretch measured on its own thread, so a wait (a leaked child holding a pipe) is judged exactly as before.
