* fix(load): a run's deadline is no longer read off a child that had already ended (GOH.PRESTEPMSGS). Two enqueue pre-steps were "killed by their deadline" at 879 s of 1500 s and 804 s of 1560 s, with `green verdict ... is kept` printed first and `no verdict` after, though neither had waited for a slot. The cause is CPython 3.15's `Popen.wait(timeout=T)` on macOS: its kqueue wait returns at the child's exit event, the child is not yet reapable, and the fallback takes the time already waited off the deadline a second time, so a child ending after more than T/2 s raised `TimeoutExpired` at the moment it ended and its real exit status was thrown away. `lib/proc_deadline.py` waits by `poll()` and lets the exit win over the clock at the deadline; `tools/load.py`, `lib/pre_deadline.py` and the five other deadline waits (`bounded_run`, `landq_spec`, `land_check`, `pin_sweep`, the MCP wire check and build) use it, and `tests/test_prestep_outcome.py` pins every other `.wait(timeout=)` in the house's code to a named grace or poll. A deadline now says one thing: load.py's line names the seconds the command RAN and the seconds it WAITED for its slot apart (the deadline counts the run only, pinned with a slot that is held first), and the pre-step wrapper no longer adds a second expiry line beside land.sh's no-verdict line.
