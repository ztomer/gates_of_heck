* GOH.APPRESTOREOWNER: the subagent-stop hook no longer relaunches a login-item app that ran at the agent's spawn and not at its stop; it names it (with the exit window) on the outcome line and to the session. The guard cannot tell an agent's quit from the owner's, and on 2026-11-23 it relaunched apps the owner had quit from the Dock to free the machine. `appstate.restore()` is now `appstate.gone()`; the `GOH_APPSTATE_LAUNCH_CMD` seam is gone with the launcher.
