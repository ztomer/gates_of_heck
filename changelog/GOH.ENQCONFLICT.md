* GOH.ENQCONFLICT: an enqueue is told of a land ahead of it that it conflicts with before its own guards and pre-step. `goh land --enqueue --land` marks its tip in flight as soon as it has one, and replays it onto main plus each land of its repo in its pre-step (its `enqueuing` marker), pending or running; a conflict is red in seconds, naming that land and what to do, and the same tip enqueued again over the same land runs (a land ahead may go red). 22 lands in 7 days went red on a conflict after 23,719 s of pre-step; replayed, 19 conflicted with a land already ahead at their enqueue (5 in its pre-step, 15 queued), 2 of the other 3 conflict no more. `goh land --wait ITEM` never answers with the run before: the background enqueue's marker is put by the process that started it, a start whose child has not marked itself in 60 s exits 75 (it was 0), a child refused before its request existed or ended by an exception is recorded red, and a wait whose enqueue ends with nothing filed exits 3.
