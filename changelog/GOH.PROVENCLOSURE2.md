- proven: closure keying is staged behind `GOH_PROVEN_CLOSURE` (`off` | `on`, any other value exit 2). Off, the default, keys every proven step on the whole goh content exactly as before GOH.PROVENCLOSURE and computes no closure (about 9.6 s a gate); `on` keys each step on its own goh closure. The default flips to `on`, in its own commit, once GOH.REUSEAUDIT samples `step` with no disagreement across 3 ships.
