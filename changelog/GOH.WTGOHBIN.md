* **A fresh lane worktree of gates_of_heck runs `./bin/goh` at once (`GOH.WTGOHBIN`).** bin/goh is a build product, so a worktree `loopctl lane claim` made had none until someone ran `scripts/build-goh.sh`, and lanes worked around it by hand. goh's own `.githooks/post-checkout` (the shim) runs `gates/hook.sh`, which builds it (`build-goh.sh --if-stale`, the shared store's copy when it has the stamp) in a gates_of_heck worktree checked out onto a branch with no bin/goh; a detached export worktree is left alone, and the hook never fails the checkout. `tests/test_hook_shim.py`.
