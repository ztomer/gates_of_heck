- `lib/advisory_db.py run -- cargo audit` applies goh's shared advisory rulings itself, per lock (`--file`/`-f`, else `./Cargo.lock`): an `--ignore` only where the blocker still reaches the advised crate, and a refusal naming a ruling whose blocker dropped it. rust_gate.sh no longer adds them separately, and a consumer's own audit step through the wrapper honours them too; a raw one refused goh v0.130.0's pin pushes on RUSTSEC-2026-0543 (GOH.AUDITONCE).
- `checks/check_raw_audit.py` (a rust_gate.sh step; `--estate DIR` ratchets `checks/raw_audit_seed.json`) refuses a `cargo audit` command outside the wrapper in GOH_CI_STEPS or a tracked tools/ script; comments and messages naming it do not count. antiknob, divoom-control, house-lib and sys_updater dropped their duplicate audit (GOH.AUDITONCE).
