* goh ship's install step only COMMITS the consumers' pins; the pushes run after the ship (GOH.PINPUSHGATE, ruling 2026-11-15). At v0.124.0 the install took 2037 s, 2010 s of it the consumers' full push gates (divoom-control 756 s, monitor 469 s, CadGoose 215 s, token-pool 184 s), against the owner's 5-10 minute bound for a whole ship; the commit-only install of v0.121.0 took 86 s, and with the commits side by side (GOH.SHIPPROFILE) the install is projected at well under 300 s. `scripts/wf_pin.py --bump` hands every pin commit ahead of its upstream to `lib/pin_sweep.py`, a sweep detached in its own session that waits for the ship to return, then pushes each consumer through its FULL push gate (never a narrower one: a pin bump changes the goh its gate runs) under `tools/load.py` (a gate's place in the load slot, a deadline, reaped by its supervisor), as many at once as the gate slots allow. Each outcome is written as it ends to `<result>.sweep.json` (pushed; refused, its gate's log kept under `<result>.sweep.pushes/`; or not pushed with why), and every outcome but pushed to the ledger `pin_push.json` beside the estate audit's state. `goh ship --status` prints the sweep's rows apart from the install's, and its profile rows as step `sweep`; the `goh wf-pin` listing now names each repo still ahead with a pin commit, with the sweep's reason and log, and exits 1; the next ship's sweep pushes it. `--sweep wait|none` runs the sweep in the foreground or not at all.
