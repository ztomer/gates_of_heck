* GOH.NOPREBATCH: `goh land --enqueue --land ... --no-pre REASON` waives the pre-step's tests for the reason it names, and no longer anything else. The enqueue's check also runs the repo's `.gatesrc` `GOH_LAND_NOPRE_STEPS` (each verbatim one of `GOH_CI_STEPS`, Rust coverage deferred; goh declares `./gates/rust_gate.sh`), so a clippy or fmt finding is refused at enqueue. A `--no-pre` land is never batched: the lander takes it alone, a batch ahead of it stops before it, and the batch window does not wait on its behalf (`lib/landq_batch.py` `alone`). GOH.NITS3's `--no-pre` land carried a clippy finding into a batch and turned two clean lands red (2026-11-23).
