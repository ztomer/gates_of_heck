* GOH.RULEINFLIGHT: a rule test (a `test_every_`/`test_no_` ratchet, a declared land guard, a tree reader or a table judge) that main gained after an item forked warns that item instead of redding it: the suite reports it xfailed with a WARNING naming the rule, the commit that brought it and that the item still owes the fix, and the land record's `waived` keeps it. The grace is one land: once a land of the item has landed under that warning, the rule reds it. A rule at the item's base, one the item adds, a new test that is no rule, or a stack where any item already holds the rule, reds as before.
