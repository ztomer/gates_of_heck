* GOH.QOSCLAMP: on macOS a lane or work gate no longer runs under `taskpolicy -c utility` (GOH.LANDSHARE). The clamp slowed idle boxes too: with 43-62% of the machine idle a CPU loop took 9.8-26.3 s against 1.05 s, and the idle suite's 17 workers took 2,117 s at 9% of a core each. The land path's share of the cores is its protection there: `GOH_LAND_SHARE` goes from 0.75 to 0.875 (14 of 16), so lanes keep 2 cores beside a land -- a 12-worker land-like job ran 5% longer than beside clamped lanes, 26% longer with the old 4 -- and a lane alone runs x2-4 faster. A run whose width was fixed at admission (a plain `load.py 0 -- CMD`) keeps it when a land starts later. `GOH_LANE_QOS=utility` asks for the old clamp by name. `nice` and the scheduler tiers are no substitute: nice 10 and 19 leave a thread's base priority at 31. Linux keeps nice + ionice, behind the same `qos_prefix` seam.
