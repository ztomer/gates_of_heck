* GOH.QOSCLAMP: on macOS a lane or work gate no longer runs under `taskpolicy -c utility` (GOH.LANDSHARE). The clamp slowed idle boxes too: with 43-62% of the machine idle a CPU loop took 9.8-26.3 s against 1.05 s, and the idle suite's 17 workers took 2,117 s at 9% of a core each. The land path's share of the cores (`GOH_LAND_SHARE`) is its protection there; `GOH_LANE_QOS=utility` asks for the old clamp by name. `nice` and the scheduler tiers are no substitute: nice 10 and 19 leave a thread's base priority at 31. Linux keeps nice + ionice, behind the same `qos_prefix` seam.
