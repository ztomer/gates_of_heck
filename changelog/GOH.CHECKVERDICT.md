* GOH.CHECKVERDICT: `goh land --check` fails closed, is recorded, and dies with its caller.
  * Its green steps and guard tests are kept for two hours by tree, base (main's tip and the fork), gate code and environment (`lib/check_verdict.py`, the `check` path of `lib/reuse.py`), and `goh land --enqueue --land` takes them whole or runs them all: an enqueue right after a green check of the same tree no longer repeats its 22.4 s (p50 of 362 runs, 33-170 s under load). A waived run, a checkout edited during the run, another main or other gates keep and take nothing; one take in ten runs anyway and is compared.
  * A SIGTERM, SIGINT, SIGHUP or SIGKILL to a check leaves no process and no registered worktree (`lib/check_jobs.py`: a watcher in its own session stops every job's whole tree, a grandchild that left its group too, and removes the commit gate's worktree). `timeout` on a check orphaned its pytest, structural and selector children.
  * Every check is a `land check` row of the timing ledger with its seconds and exit, the stopped and killed ones too; `goh land-profile --checks` reports them.
  * A test selector that fails or times out refuses the check; it answered an empty list, and the check was green on no judge.
  * The enqueue judges each commit no commit gate stamped and each item with no changelog fragment, as `--check` does, in a tree that declares goh's gates.
  * A pytest run stopped by a collection error names the error and the file it printed, `FAILED`/`ERROR` lines with no `::` are read, and a test the suite never measured is costed as one second when the runs are balanced.
