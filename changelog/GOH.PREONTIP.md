* **The enqueue judges the tip main will hold (GOH.PREONTIP).** `goh land --enqueue` rebases the tip onto main in its slot (goh's detached checkout, never the lane's tree) before the pre-step, pins the judged commit (`refs/goh/pre/<keep>`, removed with the request) and keeps `(judged, base)`; the lander still checks the slot out at the enqueued sha. A land whose main did not move since its enqueue now reuses in the pre-land (`pre_why` "reuse") and the gate's suite (`suite_reused`); one whose main moved re-judges only the readers of that move; a conflict with main is red at enqueue, before any test. A batch's gate carries from every member's kept verdict (`<record>.sources`), not the tip's alone. 9 of the last 10 lands had missed the gate's reuse with "the pre-step judged another tree or toolchain".
