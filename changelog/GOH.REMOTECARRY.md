* GOH.REMOTECARRY: nothing is weaker. A unit leaves the Mac's queue only after the reply naming it is on disk, so a controller that dies between runs it on both sides, never on neither; every share given and launched is judged as before (a dead worker, a stall, a skip mismatch or an unfinished share runs that share whole on the Mac), and one given and never launched runs on the Mac. A share red when the judges end is re-run then, before the slow tests. The Mac's pace is read from what it ran, never from what it carried, and a later share folds no width or overhead into the rates. A share's ids fit one word of the worker's command (60 KB, under Linux's 128 KiB).
