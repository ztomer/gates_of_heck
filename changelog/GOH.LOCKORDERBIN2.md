* **The lock-order check follows a call through `bin/goh`, and no lock into a detached child (`GOH.LOCKORDERBIN2`).** `checks/check_lock_order.py` saw a gate that runs `tools/landq.py` take the land lock but not one that runs `goh land`, which is the same script (`crates/goh/house_scripts.tsv`). A `goh SUB` shell command or `[goh, "SUB", ...]` argv now refers to the house script SUB runs; a call with `--detach`, `setsid` or `start_new_session=True` forms no edge, since its child holds none of the caller's locks (without that, `gates/ruff_house.py`'s detached `goh land --ruff-bump` read as 8 findings). goh's own tree stays sorted. `tests/test_check_lock_order.py`.
