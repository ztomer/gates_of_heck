* GOH.REMOTESAVED: every Linux worker share now records what it saved: the enqueue's pre-step, the lander's pre-land under the land lock, and the land gate's suite share each leave one figure (lib/remote_saved.py): the worker's host (the shard now names it first), the tests sent, the outcome, the worker's wall, the Mac side's wall, the Mac-only estimate for the share, the Mac's re-run of what came back, and the minutes saved. The lander reads them from the land's log into the record's `remote_shares`, the enqueue's sit in `pre_run.remote`, and `goh land --report` prints the minutes saved per day, by step and by host. Before, only the enqueue's share was recorded, with no time at all.
