* **The gate slots admit by class, then age, with a starvation bound (GOH.SLOTPRIORITY).** Every push gate was `ship`, ranked with a land, so on 2026-11-21 a land's gate waited 161 s behind the coordinator's `.loop` push gate and a consumer lane's `ci_hook.sh`, and earlier lands 293 s behind the pin sweep's consumer pushes. The long lane's classes are now `land` > `ship` > `lane` > `work` (`lib/gate_lanes.py`), declared by the caller (`lib/land_gate.sh` says `land`, `goh ship` says `ship`, a push gate nobody classed is `work`, the lowest), never inferred from a command line; a waiter past `GOH_GATE_SLOT_STARVE_MIN` (15, in `.gatesrc`) goes ahead of every class. The wait is recorded per class: a push row's `slot_class`, a land record's and its `land attempt` row's `slot_wait_by_class`, and `goh land-profile` shows `slot wait <class>` rows under `slot wait`.
