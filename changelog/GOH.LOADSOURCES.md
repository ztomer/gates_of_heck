- What burns the CPU is recorded: a launchd job (`com.ztomer.goh-load-sources`, `tools/install_load_sources.sh`) samples the process table every 30 s, read only and without privilege (`lib/proc_table.py`), into a daily ledger of cpu-s and rss by process family (a daemon by name, else the outermost house context in the ancestry: land, ship, push-gate, commit-gate, gate, pin-sweep, sccache; else an app bundle; else the nearest tool, `agent:`-prefixed under an agent), repo and worktree of the cwd, and executable basename, never a path or an argument. A child born and reaped between samples is counted through its parent's reaped time, less what was seen of the children that vanished. `goh load --report [--since] [--by]` ranks the families against the machine's busy cpu-s (host CPU ticks) and names the coverage and the unattributed rest; the sampler's own cost is in every line (GOH.LOADSOURCES). The sccache server and its compiles are their own family, which the timing record's cpu-s never saw.
- The push gate times its machine-wide slot wait from the ask: the push row carries `slot_wait_s` and `slot_asked_at`, and `lib/admit_replay.py` puts a push's arrival at its ask, no longer at its span's start after the slot.
- `goh load` reads a Homebrew framework Python (its executable sits in `Python.app/Contents/MacOS`) as the python or pytest it runs, not as an app: the first hour ranked 6.2% of the busy CPU as `app:Python`.
