* GOH.ADAPTBATCH: the lander sizes each batch from its repo's red rate at the gate instead of a fixed 3 (`lib/landq_size.py`). A batch of k is green only with (1-p)^k and every red bisects: on 11-23, 14 of 34 gated lands red (p 0.41), 3 per gate cost 1.90 gates per landed item where one at a time costs 1.69. k is the size that minimises expected gates per landed item at p, the share red of the repo's newest 20 attempts a gate judged (an enqueue pre-step's, a check's or a conflict's red never counts), computed exactly by running the lander's own bisect over every red/green pattern of k items: 1 at p of about 0.30 or more, 6 on a clean history, 3 with fewer than 10 on record; `GOH_LANDQ_BATCH` set still wins. The lander logs `batch size k=N: why` at each pick, each done record carries `k` and `k_why`, and `goh land --report` shows `batch size k` per row (JSON `k`: {k: batches}). `--report`'s `gates` no longer counts a land gate for an enqueue pre-step's, a check's or a conflict's red, which ran none: 11-23's 5.10 gates per landed item was 1.90.
