* `goh land --report [--since DATE] [--json]` (also `goh landq --report`): where the landing queue's wall time goes, per repo and UTC day: attempts, landed, red, queue wait, gate minutes split into slot wait and run, load, gates per landed, and the unloaded reference (the shortest green run). Every new done record now carries `started`, `finished`, `slot_wait_s`, `load_start` and `load_end`; an older record falls back to its log's birth time, and a log reused by a later attempt marks that run unknown instead of negative (`tests/test_landq_report.py`).
