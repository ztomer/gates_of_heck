* **A land's record says what its pre-step did with the lane's run of the set** (GOH.LANEPRESTEP). The enqueue's request and its done record carry `pre_affected`: a take of the lane's kept verdict (`took`, `by`, `lane_s` the seconds not repeated, `tests`) or a run of the set and why (`ran`), read back by `lib/affected_verdict.py` `outcome` from the lines it says. Measured first over 24 goh lands: the duplicate the item named was already gone where a lane ran the set on its enqueued tree (5 takes at 26-35 s; 2 doubles, the audit's shadow and a recommit since keyed by tree), and 17 had the pre-step's run alone, so `pre_s` (median 274 s) is one run, not two.
