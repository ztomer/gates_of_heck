- `goh budget [--json]`: the time budget of every subject, read from a new timing ledger (`$GOH_TIMING_DIR/ledger.jsonl`, `lib/timing_ledger.py`). Each full push gate (`<repo> push`), each land (`<repo> land`, the wait for the land lock its own field) and each ship (`<repo> ship`, and its release step `<repo> release`; every result already on disk is backfilled) appends its wall time, cpu-s and load. A subject is ARMED once a run finishes in 10 minutes or less, BREACHED when it runs over 15, CLEARED on the next run at 10 or less (commit gates 30 s / 60 s on a median of five; every threshold in one table); a regression is judged on cpu-s over the median of the runs before it, voided when the gate's own control drifted, so a loaded box does not read as a slow gate. Subjects no writer feeds yet are listed unmeasured (GOH.GATEBUDGET).
