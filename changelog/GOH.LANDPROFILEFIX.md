* **`goh land-profile` counts a land the way the SPD exit is judged (GOH.LANDPROFILEFIX).** On 2026-11-21 three hand counts of one day's lands gave 15.2, 31.0 and 41.6 min. A batch's one pre-land and gate run were counted on every member (BACKSTOPSTAMP/FSCHURN3, LANDCHECK/WAITSTOP): they now count once, on the batch's owner, while each member keeps its own e2e, enqueue pre-step and queue wait. The e2e started at the landing's request, outside a red enqueue pre-step and the time before it: it starts at the first enqueue attempt (the chain's requests and the item's enqueue logs; a new `earlier attempts` stage). `--last N` and `--since COMMIT` (lands whose landed commit descends from it) also print the one figure the exit is judged by, commit to landed (author date of the landed commit to the record's `finished`), its median, the share of lands whose lander ran the WHOLE pre-land again, the enqueue pre-step's ran/took, and its wait, run and workers, which the land record now keeps as `pre_run` (`lib/land_spd.py`).
