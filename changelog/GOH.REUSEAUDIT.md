* **The reuse auditor (GOH.REUSEAUDIT, slice 1).** Every reuse in goh is a registered path in `lib/reuse.py`'s one table, records in the store or still in their own: its key fields, its sites, its store and its shadow; the review's list is all there (`gate`, `pushed`, the goh-landed push rule `landed`, the tag push's 'the remote already has it' `origin`, `checks/_estate_cache.py`'s `estate`, the land pre-step's `test`, `affected_tests`' selection, the proven `step`, sccache/llvm-cov's `rustcov`, the per-file `file`). `lib/reuse_audit.py` samples one use in a path's `every` (counted, never by chance), compares the sample with a fresh run, re-runs both sides of a disagreement before believing it (known load flakes), and turns a repeated one's path OFF in one recorded state in the store (`<root>/audit/<path>.json`, never an environment variable), filing a fault item with both verdicts; it stays off until `reuse_audit.py enable PATH --fix SHA --test FILE::TEST` names the fix and its calibrated miss. A window that compared fewer samples than it needs is starved and turns the path off too; a thin one is never read as clean. `estate` is shadowed inline and the release push's `landed` by hosted CI's full gate of the pushed commit, read by the idle lander (`loopctl ci`); `reuse_audit.py report` counts uses, samples, disagreements and flakes per path. A planted under-keyed estate key (the corpus stat dropped) is caught by its shadow and switched off with a fault filed, and `tests/test_reuse_audit.py` refuses a reuse site no path names. The retired `GOH_REUSE_SHADOW` sample is gone. Still open: shadows for `gate`, `test`, `origin`, `step`, `rustcov` and `file`.
