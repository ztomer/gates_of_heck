* GOH.PATHINVLEAK: `gates/path_invariance.py` leaves no scratch copy behind. A TERM or HUP (a step ceiling, a closed terminal) now unwinds through its `finally`, and each copy names its owner pid in `.owner`, so the next run sweeps a copy whose run is gone (SIGKILL runs no handler) and one with no owner file once it is 6 h old. Three such copies (22 MB) were left under `~/.cache/goh`; they are gone.
