* `goh ship --status RESULT` now says where the time went (GOH.SHIPPROFILE). Each step runs with `GOH_TIMINGS` at a file beside the result (the step's `timings`), every bounded step under it records the 1-min load average at its start (`load1`), `tools/release_goh.sh` makes its loaded suite, land and pushes one row each (`step_timings.py time LABEL -- CMD`, children nested under it), and the install records one row per consumer; `--status` prints the costliest sub-steps as a table with the load beside each. Before it, v0.124.0's result said only `release 1192 s` and `install 2037 s`.
* goh ship's install step moves its consumers side by side, as many at once as the machine's gate slots (`consumer_pins.sweep`, `GOH_GATE_SLOTS`, else one per eight cores). GOH.PINPROPAGATE (v0.122.0) made the sweep push each consumer through its own push gate one after another: the install went from 86 s (v0.121.0) to 1974-2037 s (v0.122.0-v0.124.0), the sum of 12 consumer gates (v0.124.0: divoom-control 756 s, monitor 469 s, CadGoose 215 s, token-pool 184 s, the other eight 15-108 s each); two slots bring it to about the larger of the slowest consumer and half the sum, roughly 1000 s. A consumer's gates no longer inherit the ship's `GOH_TIMINGS` (`estate_commit.env`).
