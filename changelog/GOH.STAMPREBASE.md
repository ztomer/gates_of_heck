* GOH.STAMPREBASE: a rebased lane's commits are gated side by side, and a stamp is of what the gate read.
  * `goh land --check` and the enqueue run the commit gate on each unstamped commit at the share of the gate that asks (the enqueue's as the land path), each worker in one worktree it moves from commit to commit, beside the rest of the check: GOH.PYSLOW4's 18 commits took 284.9 s one by one and 50.2 s at a share of 7 (load 30 both).
  * A commit's stamp is `lib/commit_key.py`'s key, not its whole tree: the change (each touched path's modes and blobs, before and after) and every other path of the tree less the prose its gate reads none of (`changelog/*.md` fragments and `docs/` but decisions, config and the map, for a commit of code and its fragment in a goh checkout). A rebase over that prose gates nothing again.
  * A change no gate saw is no longer stamped by the tree it ends in: a `--no-verify` commit squashed into a judged one, a revert, or the whole of an amended commit is gated. Stamps from before the key stamp nothing, so a lane in flight is gated once more.
