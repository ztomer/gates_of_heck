# GOH_REMOTE_SHARE
Area: remote-worker
Kind: setting
Order: 140
Default: unset (measured)

Unset, the land pre-step sizes the worker's share from both sides' measured pace (GOH.REMOTESHARE,
`lib/remote_rates.py` `Rates`): each land records the worker's per-test seconds (goh-cache.db's
`remote_secs`), its width (junit case seconds over the suite's wall) and fixed overhead, and the
Mac's width (its estimated serial seconds over its wall) beside the duration table
(`lib/durations.py`), and the next split sends the costliest Linux-safe files while the worker's estimated wall stays
within the Mac's. A fraction `F` instead caps the worker at `F` of the set's estimated seconds (a
fixed share, for a measurement). The worker's passes never stand for the land gate: the gate and the
ship rerun them on the Mac.
